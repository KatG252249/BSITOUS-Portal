Submit a ticket on PUP SINTA under ICT Services > Request for Webmail / Information Systems Support.

Check the Dev & Lab Toolkit page on this site for verified links, or coordinate with your Course Specialist (CS) for the required software versions.

A mid-range laptop or desktop (Core i5/Ryzen 5, 8GB-16GB RAM) is recommended to comfortably run virtual machines, IDEs, database servers, and network simulators.

Academics, Curriculum, & Subject Load

You cannot enroll in the higher-level subject until the prerequisite is passed. For INC grades, you must process a Completion Form within one (1) year with your Course Specialist.

No, overloading is strictly for graduating students (max 6 units if graduating within 2 semesters, max 9 units if graduating in the current semester).

You may add back subjects using the Application for Change of Enrollment (ACE) form during the adjustment period, provided there are no schedule conflicts with your main block and you haven't exceeded your allowed unit load.

To qualify for academic honors in a given semester, students must meet the following General Weighted Average (GWA) cut-offs:

  • President’s Lister (PL): GWA of 1.00 to 1.45
  • Dean’s Lister (DL): GWA of 1.4501 to 1.75

Additional Requirements:

  • Must be carrying the prescribed regular unit load for the semester.
  • Must have no grade lower than 2.50 in any subject (including PE and NSTP).
  • Must have no Failed (5.00), Incomplete (INC), Withdrawn (W), or Dropped (D) remarks on record.
  • Must not have any accredited/transferred subjects processed within that specific semester.

Capstone & Internship (Practicum)

Preparation usually starts in your 3rd year. Title defense and system proposal take place in Capstone 1, while full development, testing, and deployment occur in Capstone 2.

Capstone topics must solve a real-world problem or business need (e.g., Web/Mobile Applications, IoT, Enterprise Systems) and must be reviewed and approved by the Capstone Committee and Program Chair.

You must complete at least 75%–80% of your academic units, pass all prerequisite major subjects, and secure endorsement forms from the OUS Placement Office.
