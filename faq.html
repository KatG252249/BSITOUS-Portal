Frequently Asked Questions

Common administrative and curricular queries for BSIT students.

Students entering the BSIT program from unrelated fields (e.g. non-tech courses or associate degrees) must take Bridging Courses (like basic computing and intro programming) before registering for core subjects. Coordinates with the Department Evaluator to evaluate your transcripts and get the official list of required bridging units. Submit the evaluation sheet during enrollment.

The standard load is 15 to 18 units per semester. Graduating students or those with high GPAs may request an Overload (up to 21 or 24 units) using the Overload Request form, subject to the approval of the Program Chair. Underload (below 12 units) requires administrative justification, especially if maintaining scholarships.

All academic assessments, lab files (e.g. Cisco configs, Git repositories, code zip packages), and online quizzes are handled via the PUP ODL LMS. Ensure you follow standard naming conventions designated by your professors (e.g., [Section]_[LastName]_Lab1.zip). Never submit links with restricted permissions.

Tuition payments are made through Landbank, GCash, or designated PUP cashier offices. After payment, scan and submit the transaction receipt to the OUS Accounting Portal. Verification typically takes 3-5 business days. Once verified, your status in the PUP SIS will update to "Enrolled".
