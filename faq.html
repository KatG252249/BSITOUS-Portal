Enrollment, Subjects, & Schedule

First-year, first-semester students are generally block-enrolled by the department. You can verify your enrolled subjects and download your Certificate of Registration (COR) directly via your SIS account under Enrolment / Registration.

BSIT OUS implements a flexible distance-learning setup where the semester is divided into Cycles:

  • Cycle-Based Schedule: Instead of taking all subjects simultaneously throughout the entire semester, your courses are distributed into scheduled cycles (e.g., Cycle 1, Cycle 2). Each cycle focuses on a specific set of subjects over a designated period.
  • Synchronous Sessions: Live lectures and discussions (held via Google Meet or MS Teams) typically take place on designated time and day according to your cycle schedule.
  • Asynchronous Learning: In between live sessions, you complete self-paced modules, readings, and laboratory activities through the PUP eMabini Portal.

You can view the complete 4-year BSIT curriculum under the Academics section of this portal or through your SIS student profile.

You may add back subjects using the Application for Change of Enrollment (ACE) form during the adjustment period, provided there are no schedule conflicts with your main block and you haven't exceeded your allowed unit load.

To qualify for academic honors in a given semester, students must meet the following General Weighted Average (GWA) cut-offs:

  • President’s Lister (PL): GWA of 1.00 to 1.45
  • Dean’s Lister (DL): GWA of 1.4501 to 1.75

Additional Requirements:

  • Must be carrying the prescribed regular unit load for the semester.
  • Must have no grade lower than 2.50 in any subject (including PE and NSTP).
  • Must have no Failed (5.00), Incomplete (INC), Withdrawn (W), or Dropped (D) remarks on record.
  • Must not have any accredited/transferred subjects processed within that specific semester.
