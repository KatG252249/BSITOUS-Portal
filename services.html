Pre-requisites & Instructions

  • Verify Prerequisites: Ensure you have met all course prerequisites before request submissions. Check the curriculum checklist.
  • Student Representative Sign-off: Prior evaluation by your Year-Level Rep is highly recommended to speed up verification.
  • Prepare Forms: Download and pre-fill the necessary PDFs (ACE forms, overload sheets) from the downloads panel.
  • Google Account Required: Booking sessions sync directly with Google Calendar. An invite containing the MS Teams / Zoom link will be sent automatically.
